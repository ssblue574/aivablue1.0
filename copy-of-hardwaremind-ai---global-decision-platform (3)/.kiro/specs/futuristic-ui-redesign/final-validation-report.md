### 1. Playwright 测试配置问题 (3个文件)

**问题**: Playwright 测试文件被 vitest 错误地执行

**失败的文件**:
- `tests/accessibility.spec.ts`
- `tests/browser-compatibility.spec.ts`
- `tests/visual-regression.spec.ts`

**错误信息**: "Playwright Test did not expect test.describe() to be called here"

**原因**: vitest.config.ts 没有排除 Playwright 测试文件
